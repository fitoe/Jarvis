# Product Validation

Software evidence proves that a system behaves as specified. Product evidence
tests whether the behavior solves a valuable problem.

Use product validation for new products, uncertain user jobs, or expensive
direction choices. Skip it for clear maintenance, bounded features, and defects.

Record only:

```yaml
value_hypothesis: "who gains what value and why"
first_user_signal: "smallest observation that could challenge the hypothesis"
feedback: "actual interview, demo, prototype, usage, or conversion evidence"
decision: "continue, adjust, or stop"
```

Prefer the cheapest credible signal. Do not demand analytics, research reports,
or statistically significant experiments for an early reversible slice.
