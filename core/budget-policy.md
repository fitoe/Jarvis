# Budget Policy

Budgets stop unproductive loops; they do not convert incomplete work into success.

For uncertain or costly slices, set only material limits:

- repair attempts before diagnosis and replanning;
- new dependencies;
- external spend;
- generated assets or model calls;
- fidelity target;
- timebox for spikes.

Defaults should remain small. When a budget is reached, classify why progress
stalled, then shrink the slice, change approach, defer nonessential work, or ask
for direction. Report the unmet claim explicitly.
