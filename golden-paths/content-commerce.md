# Content or Commerce Golden Path

## Use when

Discovery, trust, catalog content, conversion, search visibility, or purchase
intent dominates the product.

## Defaults

- Prefer server-rendered or statically generated public pages where practical.
- Treat content structure, metadata, performance, and responsive media as product
  behavior.
- Keep content ownership separate from visual composition when editors exist.
- For commerce, keep pricing and order integrity server-authoritative.
- Do not build checkout, payment, or inventory semantics from mock visuals.

## First slice

Deliver one complete discovery-to-action path: landing or listing, detail, and a
realistic conversion action.

## Verify

Check representative viewport rendering, metadata, navigation, performance risk,
and the resulting lead, cart, or order state.

## Escalate when

Payments, inventory, tax, shipping, consent, analytics, or production CMS changes
enter scope.
