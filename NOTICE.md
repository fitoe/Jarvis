# Influences

Jarvis incorporates independently written, compact principles inspired by:

- `karpathy-guidelines` for assumption visibility, simple implementations,
  surgical changes, and verifiable success criteria. The referenced skill is MIT
  licensed.
- `efficient-development-workflow` for risk-proportional process, outcome-focused
  testing, and avoiding unnecessary ceremony.

Jarvis does not vendor either skill. This repository's original content is
released under the MIT License.
